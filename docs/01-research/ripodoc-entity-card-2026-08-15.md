## Analysis implications

RipoDoc is an emerging workflow category rather than a mature reporting-software incumbent. It should therefore be assessed in two ways: as a potential **substitution or add-on** to existing reporting/portal spend, and as potential **expansion** spend for a new report-evidence workflow. Public competitor prices are context, not a valuation of RipoDoc or proof that its price ladder will clear.
