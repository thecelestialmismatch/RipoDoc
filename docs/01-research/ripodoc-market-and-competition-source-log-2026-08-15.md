## Core conclusion supported by the sources

The sources support a **problem hypothesis**, not a success proof: MSPs are encouraged to make QBRs concise, business-oriented, and grounded in operational data while also demonstrating client value. Broad competitors sell dashboards, reports, portals, automation, and business-planning tooling. RipoDoc’s opportunity is only credible if a material subset of MSPs will pay for a distinct workflow: a selected client-report claim linked to reviewed, bounded, recipient-appropriate evidence without turning the client interaction into a broad portal or raw-export handoff.
