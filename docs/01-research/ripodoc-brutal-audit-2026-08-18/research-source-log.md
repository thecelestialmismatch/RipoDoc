## Research boundaries

1. Public business counts are **not** a count of MSPs or of qualified RipoDoc buyers. They serve only as an outer universe for sensitivity framing.
2. Published competitor product pages establish advertised capability, not verified customer outcomes, revenue, or willingness to pay.
3. No public source yet establishes RipoDoc product-market fit, customer retention, or a defensible CAC/LTV distribution; those will remain explicitly estimated or unproven in the audits.
4. The research will not call RipoDoc an AI-native compliance product, compliance system, or certified solution unless the actual product boundary is expanded and separately evidenced.

## Competitor synthesis saved for audit drafting

Broad MSP systems compete for the **QBR preparation, portal, planning, asset, budget, and relationship-management job**. Generic secure-document products compete for the **secure file delivery job**. RipoDoc’s defendable wedge is narrower: **a selected claim in a familiar MSP report links to reviewed, bounded, recipient-aware support**. The thesis is credible only if customers repeatedly value this boundary enough to add a product rather than use a portal, shared drive, dashboard, or secure-document service.
