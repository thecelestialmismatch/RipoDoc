## Core product boundary

RipoDoc is **not** a general-purpose PDF extractor, RMM, PSA, BI platform, client portal, blanket compliance certification, or a promise that automation can publish without review. It is intended to improve the controlled delivery of the evidence behind a report claim while preserving review and access boundaries.

## Research guardrails

Commercial analysis must treat the pricing ladder, time-savings narrative, customer counts, market scope, and launch timing as hypotheses until validated by paid customer evidence. Legal and policy work must distinguish an implementable interface control from a finalized contractual commitment.

## Owner decisions required before a broad paid hosted launch

The final seller identity, target jurisdictions, pricing/billing/tax structure, payment processor, cancellation/refund rules, data region and retention, final subprocessors, support and SLA commitments, and healthcare posture remain required business-owner and counsel decisions. These are not gaps that software copy can responsibly fill by assumption.
