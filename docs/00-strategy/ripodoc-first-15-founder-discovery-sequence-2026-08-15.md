# RipoDoc 'First 15' Founder Discovery Sequence

**Prepared:** 2026-08-15
**Audience:** RipoDoc founder
**Objective:** Secure 15 structured discovery conversations with high-fit MSP founders to validate the report-evidence problem and refine the product wedge.
**Primary Ask:** A 15-minute "problem interview" or "workflow teardown" focused on one challenged report claim.

> **Strategic Rule:** This sequence is for learning, not selling. If you pitch the product features before understanding their specific challenged claim, you have failed the interview. The goal is to find 15 people who can name a real, recurring reporting pain point.

## 1. Targeting the First 15

Do not automate this. Select 15 founders who meet at least three of these criteria:
- They are active on LinkedIn or in MSP communities (e.g., Reddit r/msp, MSPGeek).
- Their MSP publicly emphasizes "strategic partnership," "vCIO," or "accountability."
- They have 20+ employees (likely to have a dedicated account management or reporting function).
- They have recently posted about client trust, QBRs, or reporting transparency.

## 2. Personalization Checklist

For these 15, "good enough" personalization is a disqualifier. Spend 5 minutes per person.

| Personalization Field | What to find | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |

## 4. The Discovery Call Structure (The 'Structured 15')

When you get them on the phone, use this exact sequence to validate the problem:

1.  **The "No-Sales" Reassurance (1 min):** "Thanks for the time. As promised, no pitch. I'm building this and I need to know if I'm solving a real problem or a hypothetical one."
2.  **The Context (2 mins):** "You send recurring reports. When was the last time a client asked for the detail behind a specific number?"
3.  **The Workaround (4 mins):** "What did you send them? How long did it take to prepare? Who had to do the work?"
4.  **The Friction (3 mins):** "What was the risk of sending that? Did they get more data than they needed? Did it actually answer their question?"
5.  **The Boundary (3 mins):** "If you could have sent a link that showed *only* the supporting rows for that one number, with an expiry date, would that have changed the conversation?"
6.  **The Close (2 mins):** "Based on that, should I keep building this or is the current workaround 'good enough'? Also, is there anyone else you think I should talk to?"

## 5. Success Metrics for the First 15

| Metric | Goal | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Call booked | 15 | You have identified people who care enough to talk. |
| Specific claim named | >10 | The problem is concrete, not abstract. |
| Workaround described | >10 | There is an existing cost/effort to displace. |
| Referral given | >5 | Your framing was credible enough for them to stake their reputation. |
| "Keep building" verdict | >8 | You have a mandate for the next phase. |

If fewer than 5 people can name a specific challenged claim, **stop.** The problem may not be painful enough to justify a standalone product.
