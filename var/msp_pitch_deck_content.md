## Slide 2: The MSP Reporting Dilemma
- **The Problem**: MSPs spend hundreds of hours every month compiling QBRs, security audits, and service reports into static PDFs.
- **Client Perception**: Clients view PDF reports as opaque, untrusted, or static documents, often questioning billable hours and ROI.
- **Operational Burden**: Traditional report generation requires tedious manual cross-referencing, leading to high support overhead and client churn.
- **The Stakes**: Without immutable proof of service delivery, MSPs struggle to defend premium retainers during contract renewals.

---

## Slide 3: The RipoDoc Solution
- **Zero Stack Disruption**: MSPs send the exact same PDF reports they already generate from ConnectWise, IT Glue, or custom monitoring tools.
- **Automated Evidence Layer**: RipoDoc automatically processes incoming PDFs, detects financial and security figures, and injects tamper-proof verification links.
- **Clickable Verification**: Every metric in the PDF links directly to verified underlying database records and audit logs.
- **Client Confidence**: Clients click any figure to instantly inspect raw proof, eliminating disputes and reinforcing trust.

---

## Slide 4: How It Works (The 2-Step Workflow)
- **Step 1: Propose (AI & Heuristics)**: RipoDoc analyzes the uploaded PDF report, maps figures to verified source records, and proposes precise placement coordinates.
- **Step 2: Human-in-the-Loop Review**: Account managers or technicians review proposed placements in an intuitive dashboard to guarantee 100% accuracy.
- **Step 3: Deterministic Publish**: RipoDoc injects secure, cryptographic link annotations into the PDF without altering original formatting or visual styles.
- **Guaranteed Integrity**: Pre-flight and post-flight verification ensure zero missing links or broken coordinate mappings before client delivery.
