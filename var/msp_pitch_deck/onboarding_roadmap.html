Implementation Roadmap & Onboarding
Week 1
Audit & Integration: Connect RipoDoc to your existing PSA/RMM reporting pipeline and configure template manifests.
Week 2
Pilot Rollout: Deploy verifiable reports to a select tier of high-value enterprise clients.
Week 3
Team Training: Train account managers on the review dashboard and placement verification workflow.
Week 4
Full Scale: Roll out clickable, verifiable reporting across 100% of your client base.
