# Live Website Conversion Audit

**Inspected:** 2026-08-13  
**URLs:** `https://www.ripodoc.com/`, `https://www.ripodoc.com/download`

| Severity | Surface | Verified observation | Buyer / venture implication | Required correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blocker | Hosted conversion path | The homepage leads with **Create a report** and the navigation leads to `/upload`, while hosted account actions currently fail closed because authentication is not configured. | A prospective MSP is routed to an action that cannot become a real hosted workflow; this weakens trust and invalidates conversion measurement. | Configure durable hosted identity first. Until it is active, make the primary public CTA a truthful founding-partner report diagnostic / product tour, and label self-hosted authoring accurately. |
| High | Product framing | Homepage workflow begins with **Upload a PDF**, despite research showing arbitrary PDF charts do not reliably contain source rows. | It risks buyers inferring unsupported “AI extracts any PDF” capability, which conflicts with the product’s strongest technical insight. | Lead workflow with approved structured/connected data; move overlay/PDF mode into an explicit, review-required secondary path. |
| High | Commercial path | The homepage and download page offer no paid-design-partner / report-diagnostic flow, meeting request, response expectation, or measurable lead path. | The site cannot generate the paid customer evidence required for YC readiness. | Add a privacy-safe founding-partner inquiry flow, anti-spam control, confirmation, internal lead record, source attribution, and explicit design-partner scope. |
| High | Hosted vs self-host clarity | The download page is operationally strong but does not visibly help a buyer decide among hosted evidence experience, self-hosted full stack, and private worker operation. | Operators may misunderstand what the public Vercel site can safely host and what must remain in controlled infrastructure. | Add a decision matrix and architecture explainer distinguishing hosted workspace, self-hosted deployment, and private authoring worker. |
| Medium | Credibility | The public site is visually polished and uses a real sample interaction, but has no customer proof or founder-led proof program. | It is correct not to fabricate social proof; however, buyers and YC will need measurable proof rather than only feature claims. | Add only validated proof (e.g., real product boundaries, public security/architecture facts), plus a design-partner path and evidence dashboard. Do not add fake testimonials or metrics. |
| Medium | CTA hierarchy | “Create a report,” “Explore the live interaction,” and “Download & self-host” compete without an audience-specific decision. | Both a buyer evaluating the outcome and an operator evaluating a deployment receive a fragmented next step. | Use persona-aware paths: **See a QBR proof experience**, **Apply as a founding partner**, and **Self-host for controlled operations**. |
| Medium | Investor narrative | Current copy describes the product accurately but does not articulate the narrow wedge, market threat, expansion thesis, or proof gates. | This is not a YC application issue by itself, but it leaves the public story unable to evidence why RipoDoc can become more than an add-on feature. | Add a research-backed claim register and separate investor/founder memo; avoid speculative TAM or outcome claims on the website. |
| Low | Product tour | The sample report shows evidence detail successfully, but it does not visibly walk through approval, recipient proof, expiry/revocation, or audit result as a sequential story. | The full differentiated system is harder to understand in the first minute. | Add an interactive, non-sensitive proof tour that makes each control and customer benefit explicit. |
| Low | Documentation discovery | Strong runbooks, SBOM procedures, and a migration guide exist but are primarily linked as source documents. | Sophisticated operators can find them; commercial buyers may not understand their relevance. | Summarize verified deployment guarantees and link to detailed documentation from a clear security/operations page. |

## Positive findings to preserve

The live homepage presents a concrete sample report rather than a generic AI visual, accurately emphasizes recipient scoping, expiry, revocation, human approval, and auditability, and uses a coherent RipoDoc visual system. The self-hosted page provides a real source bundle, a Compose start, hardening content, and operational documentation. These strengths should be preserved while the funnel and hosted operation become real.

| Pass | `/dashboard` | The unauthenticated workspace route redirected to `/login`, and no workspace data was visible. | The existing tenant boundary is behaving safely. | Preserve this behavior; after hosted configuration, test the complete verified account lifecycle rather than weakening the redirect. |
